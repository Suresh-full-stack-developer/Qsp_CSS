Tirupattur

District in Tamil Nadu

State in India

Tirupattur district stands out as a culturally and economically rich region in Tamil Nadu, famously heralded as the "Sandalwood City" due to its rich history in harvesting and trading aromatic sandalwood. Beyond its forests, the district is globally celebrated for its mouthwatering Ambur Biryani and serves as a powerhouse industrial hub for premium leather manufacturing and footwear exports. Nature and science thrive side-by-side here, home to the scenic Yelagiri hill station, the cascading Jalagamparai Waterfalls, and the renowned Vainu Bappu Observatory in Kavalur. Crucially, the district's connectivity is anchored by Jolarpettai Junction (JTJ), one of the most vital railway transit hubs in South India that seamlessly links Chennai, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, and Kerala.
